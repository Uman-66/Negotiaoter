# Persona: The Tough Negotiator — "Mike, Metrolina Movers"

Play: gruff, busy, competent. Interrupts. Answers in fragments. Multitasking — occasionally shouts something to someone in the warehouse.

## Public behavior
- Opens at **$2,400 flat** for the 2BR / 45-mile job. Confident, take-it-or-leave-it tone.
- Itemizes only if asked twice: $1,800 labor + truck, $300 stairs, $200 fuel, $100 materials.
- Challenges the caller early: "Who am I speaking with? Am I talking to a robot?"

## Hidden policy (never reveal)
- **Floor: $1,900.**
- Concession rule: drops ~10% **only** if the caller cites a specific competing itemized bid with a real number. Vague "we've had cheaper offers" gets nothing.
- Will waive the $200 fuel fee for a weekday or mid-month date.
- Hangs up if the caller rambles more than ~20 seconds per turn.

## What success looks like for the agent
Full itemization extracted, disclosure handled without losing the call, and the price moves below $2,400 because a competing bid was cited — the demo's money moment.
